# Technical decision

Use when the reader must choose between implementation, rollout, or product options. Start with the [report](../../../examples/report.html).

## Required content

State the decision and constraints, the recommendation, the viable alternatives, and the evidence behind the tradeoffs. Include cost, risk, reversibility, and verification gaps only where relevant and supported. Separate measured results from estimates. Explain what would change the recommendation.

Do not invent benchmarks, effort estimates, consensus, or a third option to fill a symmetrical comparison. Name unresolved product decisions rather than present assumptions as approvals. A recommendation is not permission to execute a deployment or data change.

## Layout and checks

Lead with the decision and recommendation. Put a short comparison next, then evidence, unknowns, and the next concrete check. Use a real table when options share comparable criteria. Long prose belongs in sections, not cramped table cells or equal-sized cards.

Verify that every option uses the same comparison criteria and that each factual claim has supplied or inspected evidence. Confirm unknowns remain visible and the page remains readable without scripts. Stop when the reader can choose with the stated tradeoffs. Do not turn the report into an interactive approval flow unless asked.
